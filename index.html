Simple Process

Three steps to get started.

If you've already purchased the software, the process is usually easier when you approach it in the right order.

01

Start With Your Account

Begin by identifying the account associated with your existing purchase. Make sure you have access to the email address and sign-in information you normally use.

02

Download & Install

Use the appropriate official download source for your software and operating system. Then follow the installation instructions displayed on your computer.

03

Complete Activation

Once installation is complete, sign in or provide the requested purchase information and finish the activation process.

Can't remember where to begin?

Start with the guide that best matches your situation. If you've already purchased the software, the existing customer and reinstall guides are usually the best starting points.
